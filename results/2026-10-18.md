# Reducto table self-check on RD-TableBench, 2026-10-18

100 public RD-TableBench PDFs parsed by Reducto with ocr_system=combined, the setting Reducto's own benchmark uses, and graded with rd-tablebench's own `table_similarity`. Mean score 0.581.
A parse counts as bad below 0.5: 35 of 100 (35%).

| Metric, 5-fold CV | Value |
|---|---|
| AUC, bad vs good parses | **0.708** |
| Tables flagged to catch 80% of bad parses | 67 of 100 (67%) |
| Share of flagged tables that are bad | 42% vs 35% if flagged at random, 1.2x |

The self-check reads only the predicted table, never the ground truth, so a pipeline can run it in production.

## Honest limits

- Only the public subset of RD-TableBench; Reducto holds the full set back to prevent contamination.
- A logistic model on hand-picked structural features. It shows the signal exists, not the ceiling.
- An AUC near 0.5 means no better than chance. The lift row is the honest test: flagging at random already catches bad parses at the base rate.
- The 0.5 cutoff is a choice. Per-table scores are saved in results/scores-combined.json so you can pick another.
